A counting game for the dark wood

Firefly Gates

Lead your fireflies through the dark wood. Every gate does sums to them. Gather enough light to wake the beacon.

  • Steer with the mouse, a finger, or ← →.
  • Each row of gates does a sum to your swarm +−×÷. Fly through the one that leaves you more.
  • Dodge the moths. At the end, wake the beacon: bring at least the number cut into it.
