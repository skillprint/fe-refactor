A field guide to twins

Mountain Appreciator

Two mountains, nearly identical. Find what makes each one itself before the light goes.

  • Drag to walk around them. Scroll or pinch to lean in.
  • Click a difference on either mountain. Wrong guesses cost daylight.
  • Marks at the top right show what kind of thing differs: trees, water, sky, wildlife, rock or weather.
  • Hold Space to blink between the twins. The sun moves faster while you do.
