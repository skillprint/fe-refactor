A mobile, out of balance

Equi­librium

Hang the weights so every arm rests level.

  • Each beam pivots at its middle. Weight × distance must match on both sides.
  • Drag a weight from the shelf onto a hook, or tap it, then tap a hook. Tap a hung weight to take it back.
  • A beam hanging from a hook weighs everything below it.
  • Hang every loose weight before the candle burns down.
