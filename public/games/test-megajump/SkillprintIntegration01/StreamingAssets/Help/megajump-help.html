How to Play Mega Jump

The goal of Mega Jump is to jump as high as you can and reach new Stages!

 

To move your character left or right, just tilt your device.

 

To jump higher, your character must collect coins and other items. If you don't catch something, you will fall and it'll be Game Over!

How to Get More Mega Points

There are lots of things that you can do to get more MP. Here are a few:

  1. You get 10MP for every 100 coins you collect.
  2. Upgrade the Magnet! The Magnet is one of the best ways to get more coins during the game, so it's worth spending MP on.
  3. Upgrade the Action Umbrella! The Turbo, Super and Mega Action Umbrella act like magnets when you are falling, pulling up coins from below. Not only that, but a rain of coins will fall, speeding up your coin collecting!
  4. Play the lower Stages. Stages 1 and 2 are filled with coins and not many obstacles, so they are a great place to earn MP.

Boosts and Characters

The Boosts section gives you access to lots of new characters, items, powerups and special features to unlock.

 

You unlock items by using Mega Points (MP), which you can earn by playing the game.

 

You can get to the Boosts section by pressing the Boosts button found on the Main Menu and Game Over screens.

Mega Points

For every 100 coins you collect in the game you will earn 10 Mega Points (MP).

Save Stars

You start with 5 Save Stars, and you can purchase more Save Stars in the store using MP.

 

Save Stars can be used in the game at any time, and are particularly useful when you are falling. If you have a Save Star, just tap the Save Star button in the bottom left of the screen when you fall, and you'll boost back up on a trail of stars!

 

Each Save Star can only be used once though, so use them wisely!

Gold Coins

Collect gold coins whenever you see them! Both the small and large coins have the same value.

 

TIP: Collect 100 gold coins and you'll get a boost and 10 Mega Points (MP) to spend in the Store!

x2 Coins

The gold coins with the Blue center are worth 2 regular gold coins!

x5 Coins

The gold coins with the Red center are worth 5 regular gold coins!

Trickster Coins

Watch out for this sneaky character! He looks a lot like the precious gold coins but will run away as soon as you get near him!

Moving Coins

The red coins move from side to side across the screen making them harder to catch.

 

TIP: Aim for groups of moving coins to increase your chance of catching one!

Powerups

Powerups are items in the game that do something special when you pick them up.

 

Many of the powerups are upgradable. Upgradable powerups can be made more powerful by unlocking new upgrade levels in the Store. There are four levels for each powerup.

 

When you unlock a new level for a powerup, that will replace the current version that you have.

 

TIP: Some powerups are better for collecting coins, some are better for sending you higher and faster, and some are better for scoring more points, so choose based on what you want to do!

Boost Stars

Boost stars are plentiful in the world of Mega Jump. Collecting one of these stars will give you a boost. Stars come in many colors, each one more powerful than the last: Yellow, Green, Blue and the very boosty Red Star.

Action Umbrella 1

With this powerup, you'll float gently down when you fall, giving you more time to catch coins!

Action Umbrella 2

With this upgrade, a shower of blue coins will start to fall! The blue coins won't boost you, but they count as real coins towards your coin total! You'll also pull 3 coins out of every coin below you like a hovering UFO!

 

TIP: Hover over large groups of coins to get the most out of this powerup!

Action Umbrella 3

With this upgrade, you will become a floating vacuum, pulling 4 coins out of every coin below you, and the blue coin rain will fall much harder.

Action Umbrella 4

The coin rain becomes a coin storm with this upgrade, and you'll pull in 7 coins for every coin caught in your Mega Beam!

Anti-Gravity Boots 1

The Anti-Gravity boots allow you to run on the side of the screen and bust through obstacles with a gravity shield!

 

TIP: Tap on the screen once to jump and collect items or tap twice to flip to the other side!

Anti-Gravity Boots 2

You will run faster, carry a larger shield and you'll also get a boost up the screen when jumping from one side of the screen to the other!

 

TIP: Double-tap on the screen to boost your character!

Anti-Gravity Boots 3

You’ll be like a charging bull when you strap these on! The gravity field is so powerful that enemies and blocks will be thrown aside and destroyed!

 

TIP: Double-tap on the screen to boost your character!

Anti-Gravity Boots 4

All objects in your way will be crushed under the pressure of your gravity field and you'll boost so fast you'll come close to flying off the screen!

 

TIP: Double-tap on the screen to boost your character!

Balloon 1

The balloon will make you bigger, but you’ll also be as light as a feather. You will float gently upwards, plowing through enemies and other obstacles along the way!

 

TIP: When you have the balloon, tap on your character to let some air out and get a boost!

Balloon 2

This upgrade makes you even bigger and adds an orbiting sidekick, who will revolve around you, picking up extra coins and stars!

 

TIP: When you have the balloon, tap on your character to let some air out and get a boost!

Balloon 3

As you float upwards you'll get extra friends (up to a maximum of 4) and when you tap the balloon, you'll boost even higher!

 

TIP: When you have the balloon, tap on your character to let some air out and get a boost!

Balloon 4

You'll be like a floating armada with this Mega upgrade! You'll start with 4 sidekicks who will be joined by 2 more as you climb, until you have 6 orbiting friends!

 

TIP: When you have the balloon, tap on your character to let some air out and get a boost!

Jump Boots 1

These boots make every jump bigger and they even amplify Boost Stars for bigger, faster jumps!

Jump Boots 2

Every jump is bigger, and you can string together coin combos to get super boosts!

Jump Boots 3

This Super upgrade makes each bounce feel like you’re getting shot out of a cannon! Hang on!

 

TIP: Grab multiple coins in a row to activate another boost and keep your speed up!

Jump Boots 4

Strapping on these boots is like standing on a keg of dynamite! At full speed you’ll become a furious fireball, rocketing across the sky!

 

TIP: Grab multiple coins in a row to activate another boost and keep your speed up!

Lucky Blast 1

Turn obstacles into opportunities and good things into great things! When you collect this powerup, all coins, blocks, and enemies on screen will be magically converted into 2X coins or Boost Stars!

Lucky Blast 2

All objects on screen as well as those ahead of you will be converted into 2x Coins, 5X Coins, or bigger Boost Stars!

Lucky Blast 3

This powerup is so strong that objects will continue to turn into 2X Coins, 5X Coins, or big Boost stars for a full 3 seconds!

Lucky Blast 4

This upgrade gives out only the very best! All items are converted into either 5X coins or Red Boost Stars for an incredible 5 seconds! Let it ride!

Magnet 1

When you have the magnet, you'll be irresistible, pulling in any coins and stars that are near you!

Magnet 2

This upgrade will pull in coins faster and from a greater distance. Collect coins quicker than ever before!

 

TIP: With the Turbo Magnet and above, as long as you're moving upwards, you won't be hurt by spike blocks or enemies!

Magnet 3

This magnet attracts coins at super speed with a force so strong it will pull in enemies and obstacles for extra points!

Magnet 4

Become a black hole, pulling in everything around you! This magnet is so strong that all coins double in value as they shoot towards you!

Power Shield 1

Protect yourself from enemies and obstacles - by busting through them! The Shield will wrap you in an energy ring that will protect you from harm.

 

TIP: You can destroy any obstacle or enemy you touch with the Power Shield for extra points and a small boost!

Power Shield 2

Lightning bolts will shoot from your shield to destroy enemies and obstacles that come near you! All objects destroyed with the shield will get you double the regular points! The Shield lasts for 15 seconds!

 

TIP: The Turbo Shield has a short range, so get close to objects to zap them!

Power Shield 3

For 20 seconds, channel the power of storms and send lightning bolts further and faster! All items destroyed with the Shield are worth 5 times the regular points!

Power Shield 4

Nothing escapes the fury of this mega shield! You will become a buzzing wall of electricity as you earn 10 times the regular points for everything you destroy!

The Fireball

The fireball sends you soaring upward on a ball of flame and smoke. You’ll barrel through coins, powerups and enemies - nothing can stop you!

The Supernova

Hang on tight for this one! The Supernova will have you rocketing at such huge speeds that all objects will be drawn to you like moths to a Supernova!

The Anvil

The Anvil is a block of solid iron that will stop you in your tracks! Avoid this item or it will cost you your coins and may even send you falling to your doom!

 

TIP: If you hit an obstacle, quickly tilt as far as you can to catch any coins that you have lost. This will boost you back up again and stop you from falling!

Ball & Chain

If you accidentally pick up the Ball and Chain, it will clamp itself to your leg and drag you down with it! The extra weight will make you heavier and you’ll find it harder to get that next coin!

Lightning Cloud

The Lightning Cloud can spoil all your fun. If collected it will automatically remove any powerup you are currently using!

Shrinkify Potion

You'll be in big trouble with this little potion! In a puff of green vapor, you'll shrink to a quarter of your normal size, so you'll need to act fast to avoid falling through the gaps! But maybe there are places only a small monster can go?...

Kabluey

This blue bad guy has a big appetite. He'll move horizontally across the screen in an attempt to take a bite out of you. Jump on top of him to finish him off and get some well deserved points!

Purple Nurple

This purple punk flies up and down on the screen trying to block your progress and spoil your fun. Jump on his head to get him out of the way and earn some points too!

Dredful

This red rascal is big and he's lazy. He won't move to get his lunch but he'll open his huge mouth instead to bare his big nasty teeth. Show him you're the boss by jumping on him and collecting a bunch of points!

Bounce Blocks

These blocks will send you bouncing up into the air but won’t disappear after you’ve touched them.

 

TIP: You can bounce on these blocks as much as you like, so take your time!

Spike Blocks

Avoid these pointy spikes or you will lose your coins and find yourself falling into danger!

 

TIP: You can destroy spike blocks with the Power Shield, Anti-Gravity Boots and the Balloon for extra points!

Break Blocks

Break Blocks often hide treasure inside them. Bounce once on a Break Block to look into it for items and then jump one more time to break them open and collect what’s inside!

 

TIP: Be careful with Break Blocks! Hit them twice and they will disappear, leaving nothing below you!

Stages

There are many stages in Mega Jump. When you reach the end of a stage you will unlock the next one and you will see a "Stage Complete!" message!

 

Once a stage has been unlocked you can play it at any time by selecting it from the Stage Select screen. Each stage gets harder than the previous stage with more challenges and enemies to overcome.

 

When you lose a game, you will start from the last Stage that you reached.

 

Worlds

You can change which World you would like to play from the Stage Select screen. Each World has 10 easy stages and 10 Hard stages.
